## Review notes

- Wizard Tower: solid black window panes should become outlined, colorable regions at conversion.
- Walking Tree: simplify fine bark lines and inspect the nose contour before tracing.
- Several humanoid faces and hairstyles are similar; additional future variants can broaden facial and age diversity.
- Some sources use fine secondary lines on hair, armor, scales, feathers, wood grain and page edges. Inspect which details survive tracing and simplify as needed.
- Review intentional negative spaces between wings/limbs, hands and held props, handles, and folded clothing when authoring cutouts.
- All sources are PNG artwork only; normal line/region cleanup and app registration remain pending.

Pack names are scene themes. Proposed subject categories remain Characters, Animals, Buildings and Objects, with fantasy and role tags suitable for later cataloging. Original independent designs were requested rather than copies of named franchise characters.

## Conversion status

All 48 sources are converted and registered under the `fantasy-` ID prefix. The source gallery remains useful for comparison; browse the final SVGs in [the asset catalog](../../catalog/index.html). Earlier generation-time pending notes above are superseded by this status.
